The +41 kB is the counter module (f3h7.rs, 428 lines) and the `--mode h7`
entry point.  Neither is reachable from the quotient path: the counters are
behind `Option<&Counters>`, which is `None` in every timed run, and the mode
is an unused match arm.  Recorded because it is a real difference between the
two binaries, not because it is expected to matter -- and because the one
stage that moved in the wrong direction (`synthesis`, +3.0%) does not touch
the evaluator, which is what a layout effect looks like.
