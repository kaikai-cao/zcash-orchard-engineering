## What each line means

    F4A_CS     {"k":6,"ext_len":512,"gates":3,"constraints":10,
                "chains":0,"chains_shared":0,"consumers":0,
                "saved_muls":0,"occ_muls":0,"root_factorable":0,
                "identity_fail":0,"domains":1}
    F4A_CHAIN  one line per distinct recovery chain (only in the `hi` variant)
    F4A_OCC    one line per (chain, gate, poly) occurrence (suppressed by grep here)
    XCENSUS_RUN proof bytes and FNV-1a fingerprint of the produced proof

`k` in `F4A_CS` is the `Params` k (6 here), not the chain length. The chain
length is the `k` field inside `F4A_CHAIN` (3 in the `hi` variant).
